# AquaLoop AI — Every Drop Has Another Purpose

A browser-only prototype for responsible household reuse exploration across AC condensate, RO purifier reject water, and washing-machine discharge.

## What the site now includes
- **Challenge problem alignment:** explicitly maps the product concept to the water-reuse problem statement.
- **Source-to-use decision guide:** a cautious rules-based pathway that helps identify questions and red flags. It is not AI-backed water testing, certification, or a safety guarantee.
- **Water tracker and home audit:** records household entries and displays a transparent, descriptive breakdown.
- **Collection safety:** supports responsible storage and standing-water checks; it does not claim to prevent dengue or other diseases.
- **Community pilot lab:** stores anonymous, consent-based observations with measurement-method labels and CSV export.
- **User research & alternatives:** logs real interviews/observations and lays out alternatives to investigate. Nothing is prefilled as if it were real research.
- **Cost & scale model:** a simple assumption-driven calculator for setup, maintenance, displaced volume, and scenario economics. Outputs are not quotes or validated savings.
- **Challenge readiness:** a self-reported evidence checklist for problem validation, pilot observations, measurement quality, alternatives, costs, and safety review. It is not an official competition score.
- **Impact & method:** separates recorded reuse from net savings and verified impact.

## How to run
Open `index.html` in a modern browser. The app stores entries in that browser's `localStorage`; there is no account, cloud sync, backend, or real AI API. Export CSVs before clearing browser data.

## Responsible-use limits
- Never use untreated household wastewater for drinking, cooking, food preparation, or ingestion.
- Source characteristics, contamination, local requirements, intended use, storage, and appropriate testing matter. Do not infer safety from the questionnaire.
- RO reject water may have elevated dissolved salts/minerals; laundry discharge may contain detergents, lint, microbes, or other residues; AC condensate can be affected by collection surfaces and drain conditions.
- Enter only genuine research/pilot observations and keep participant data anonymous. The browser-local demo is not a secure research database.
- Do not claim verified water savings, disease reduction, environmental impact, uniqueness, adoption, or field validation without suitable evidence.

## Evidence needed before competition claims
1. Conduct and document consent-based conversations with intended households and relevant practitioners.
2. Research current workarounds and comparable solutions; cite sources and avoid unverified uniqueness claims.
3. Pilot with a consistent baseline/follow-up protocol, clearly labelling measured, reported, and estimated values.
4. Seek qualified advice and appropriate testing for source-specific water quality and intended uses.
5. Replace calculator assumptions with local quotes and actual displacement/adoption data; report limitations.

The new evidence workflow helps organise this work; it cannot replace doing the research or prove that the project will receive a particular score.
