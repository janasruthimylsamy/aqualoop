# AquaLoop AI 💧
### Every Drop Has Another Purpose.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://janasruthimylsamy.github.io/aqualoop/

## About the Project
AquaLoop AI is a water-reuse initiative that explores how water discharged from air conditioners (ACs), RO purifiers, and washing machines can be assessed for suitable non-drinking uses instead of being wasted.

My goal is to make household water reuse easier to understand through practical guidance, safety awareness, and simple digital tools.

## The Problem
Water from everyday household activities often goes unused. However, people may not know which sources could be reused, what precautions are necessary, or how to estimate the potential benefits.

## My Solution
AquaLoop AI brings water-reuse assessment, usage tracking, household water audits, safety checklists, and educational resources together in one website. It helps users explore possible reuse opportunities while keeping safety and responsible water management in focus.

## What Makes AquaLoop AI Worth Considering?
- **Everyday relevance:** Focuses on water sources found in ordinary households.
- **Practical approach:** Connects awareness with actionable guidance and tracking tools.
- **Safety-first thinking:** Recognizes that different water sources require different precautions.
- **Potential to grow:** Can be improved through household research, pilot studies, and user feedback.
- **Accessible solution:** A web-based prototype that people can explore without specialized equipment.

## Tech Stack
HTML • CSS • JavaScript

## My Vision
I want to develop AquaLoop AI into a more evidence-based solution that helps households understand water reuse, measure actual reuse practices, and make responsible decisions. The next step is to validate its usefulness through user research and real-world pilot studies.

## Safety Note
The website provides educational decision support, not water-quality certification. Water must be assessed for its source, possible contamination, and intended use. Untreated household wastewater must never be used for drinking or food preparation.
